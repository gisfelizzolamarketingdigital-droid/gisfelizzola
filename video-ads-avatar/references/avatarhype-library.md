## A. La imagen

Retoques de imagen: partes de una foto de tu avatar y la ajustas. De aquí sale la imagen que luego animarás en la parte B.

### Cambiar vestuario y fondo

**Cambio de vestuario** (foto)
```
Using this image as reference, keep the SAME person – same face, hair, body, pose, background and lighting. Change ONLY their clothing to [escribe el outfit aquí]. Keep everything else exactly the same. Photorealistic, natural fabric, realistic fit and lighting.
```

**Cambiar el fondo** (foto)
```
Replace the background behind the person with [describe el sitio: a cozy kitchen / a modern office / a neutral studio with soft lighting], keeping the person, their pose, lighting and framing exactly the same. Natural, realistic integration.
```

**Outfit por referencia** (foto · 2 imágenes)
```
Using the two reference images (image 1: the person; image 2: the outfit / clothing), keep the SAME person from image 1 – same face, hair, skin, body and identity – and dress her in the EXACT outfit shown in image 2, matching its garments, colors, patterns, textures and details as closely as possible. Change ONLY her clothing; keep her pose, framing, background and lighting exactly the same. The outfit fits her body realistically, with natural fabric folds and realistic lighting. The person is image 1, the clothes are image 2 – do NOT copy the face or body from image 2. Photorealistic. Single image.
```

**Fondo por referencia** (foto · 2 imágenes)
```
Using the two reference images (image 1: the person; image 2: the background / setting), keep the SAME person from image 1 – same face, hair, skin, body, outfit and pose – and place them into the EXACT setting shown in image 2, matching its location, colors, depth and atmosphere. Keep the person's pose, scale and framing exactly the same; replace ONLY what is behind them. Blend naturally: consistent lighting and shadows, matching color temperature and perspective, realistic edges with no cut-out look. The person is image 1, the background is image 2 – do NOT take any person or object from image 2. Photorealistic. Single image.
```

## B. El vídeo

Las piezas para animar la imagen que ya has creado y darle voz.

### Cómo se monta el prompt

La estructura base. Juntas estas piezas en este orden (la acción es opcional):

| # | Pieza | Ejemplo |
|---|-------|---------|
| 1 | Cámara · un plano, o cámara quieta | (static camera, no movement) realistic arm movements and subtle micro-movements. |
| 2 | Acción · un gesto (opcional) | She smiles and holds up the product, |
| 3 | Guión · lo que dice | then says: "Llevaba meses buscando algo así y mira, lo encontré." |
| 4 | Voz · del personaje | Voice: a light, lively female voice — a woman in her mid-20s, bright and clear... |
| 5 | Acento · si no es en inglés | Spanish with a neutral Peninsular (Madrid) accent... |

### Los movimientos de cámara

Para darle vida al plano. Los de abajo ya llevan la instrucción para que el modelo no meta un corte.
En cristiano: zoom in/out = acercarse/alejarse · travelling = desplazarse de lado · orbit = girar alrededor · handheld = leve temblor · locked-off/static = cámara quieta.

**Los básicos · empieza por estos**

- **El más usado · cámara quieta**: `(static camera, no movement) realistic arm movements and subtle micro-movements. Single continuous take, no cuts or scene changes.`
- **Plano fijo**: `Locked-off static shot, camera completely still on a tripod, no movement at all. Single continuous take, no cuts or scene changes.`
- **Zoom lento a la cara**: `Slow, continuous zoom-in toward the subject's face, starting from the very first frame and moving steadily closer, then holding steady on the close-up for the rest of the clip. Single continuous take, no cuts or scene changes.`
- **Zoom rápido al hablar**: `Fast zoom-in to the subject's face at the very start, settling into a close-up exactly as they begin to speak, then holding steady. Single continuous take, no cuts or scene changes.`
- **En mano sutil**: `Subtle handheld movement, very slight and natural, for a real human feel without distraction. Single continuous take, no cuts or scene changes.`

**Para variar**

- **Acercamiento sutil**: `Very gentle, almost imperceptible slow push-in on the speaker, from a medium shot to a medium close-up over the whole clip. Single continuous take, no cuts or scene changes.`
- **Retroceso que revela**: `Slow pull-back from a close-up, revealing the surrounding environment as the subject starts talking, then holding on the wider shot. Single continuous take, no cuts or scene changes.`
- **Órbita lenta**: `Slow arc shot, the camera orbiting around the subject in a smooth half-circle, subject stays centered. Single continuous take, no cuts or scene changes.`
- **Seguimiento al caminar**: `Tracking shot following alongside the subject as they walk and talk, smooth and steady. Single continuous take, no cuts or scene changes.`

**Avanzados**

- **Push-in a los ojos**: `Slow creeping push-in that tightens into an extreme close-up on the eyes right as the key line is delivered, then holds. Single continuous take, no cuts or scene changes.`
- **Contrapicado heroico**: `Low-angle hero shot looking up at the subject, slow push-in for authority and impact, then holding. Single continuous take, no cuts or scene changes.`
- **Profundidad de campo**: `Shallow depth of field with a creamy bokeh background, slow push-in on the in-focus subject, then holding. Single continuous take, no cuts or scene changes.`
- **Cámara lenta**: `Slow-motion delivery, smooth and cinematic, with a subtle camera drift. Single continuous take, no cuts or scene changes.`

> ¿Montas un plano tuyo? Para que no te meta un corte a media frase, pega esta línea al final (los de arriba ya la llevan):
> `single continuous take, no cuts or scene changes — the camera holds steady on the framing after the move`

### Las micro-acciones · gestos

Un gesto natural que da realismo. Ojo: es un gesto, no es lo que dice (eso es el guión).
La regla: la acción de *antes* de hablar va delante del `says:`; la de *mientras* habla va detrás.

**Antes de hablar** (vídeo)
```
The person [acción], then looks at the camera and says: "[guión]"
```

**Mientras / después de hablar** (vídeo)
```
The person says: "[guión]" [acción]
```

Lista de gestos para `[acción]`:

| Español | Inglés |
|---|---|
| se ajusta la gorra | adjusts their cap |
| sorbo de café | takes a sip of coffee and sets the cup down |
| se arregla el pelo | fixes their hair |
| saluda con la mano | waves hello |
| se ríe | laughs |
| cara de sorpresa | with a surprised look on their face |
| asiente, sonriendo | and nods slowly, smiling |
| señala hacia arriba | points up |
| señala a la izquierda | points up to the left |
| señala a la derecha | points to the right |
| cruza los brazos | crosses their arms |
| se encoge de hombros | shrugs |
| aprobación con el pulgar | gives a thumbs up |
| da una palmada | claps once |
| inclina la cabeza, curiosa | tilts their head curiously |
| se ajusta las gafas | adjusts their glasses |
| pelo detrás de la oreja | tucks hair behind their ear |
| se toca la barbilla | taps their chin thoughtfully |
| chasquea los dedos | snaps their fingers |
| ojos en blanco, en broma | rolls their eyes playfully |

> Puedes combinar las dos: `The person adjusts their cap, then says: "[guión]" and smiles.`

### La voz

Usa la misma línea `Voice:` en todos los clips del mismo personaje, generados en la misma sesión.

- **Hombre mayor · ~50**: `Voice: a deep, warm male voice – a man in his early-to-mid 50s, low and resonant with a slight gravelly texture, calm and unhurried, confident and grounded.`
- **Hombre joven · 25-30**: `Voice: a bright, energetic male voice – a guy in his mid-to-late 20s, medium-high pitch, clear and relaxed, friendly and upbeat, casual conversational pace.`
- **Hombre · mediados de 20**: `Voice: a youthful male voice – a guy in his mid-20s, bright and fairly high-pitched, light and energetic, casual relaxed pace.`
- **Hombre · mediados de 30**: `Voice: a male voice in his mid-30s, medium pitch, a bit fuller and more settled than a twenty-something, calm and confident, friendly but grounded.`
- **Mujer mayor · ~50**: `Voice: a warm, mature female voice – a woman in her early 50s, rich and slightly smoky lower-mid tone, calm and measured pacing, confident and reassuring.`
- **Mujer joven · 25-30**: `Voice: a light, lively female voice – a woman in her mid-20s, bright and clear with a higher pitch, warm and friendly, natural conversational tone.`
- **Mujer · mediados de 20**: `Voice: a youthful female voice – a woman in her mid-20s, bright and fairly high-pitched, light and breezy, casual relaxed pace.`
- **Mujer · mediados de 30**: `Voice: a female voice in her mid-30s, medium pitch, a bit fuller and more settled than a twenty-something, calm and confident, warm but grounded.`

> Crea cualquier voz combinando: género + edad (in his/her 20s / 40s / 60s) + gravedad (deep, low o bright, high) + textura (gravelly, raspy / smooth, clean/soft, breathy) + entrega (calm and slow / energetic and fast / warm / authoritative).

### El idioma y los acentos

Añade una de estas líneas al final de cualquier prompt que lleve voz.

- **España · Madrid neutro**: `Spanish with a neutral Peninsular (Madrid) accent, informal and conversational, like a real casual chat in Spain.`
- **Argentina · rioplatense**: `Spanish with an Argentine Rioplatense (Buenos Aires) accent, including the typical "sh" sound for "ll" and "y".`
- **Colombia · bogotano**: `Spanish with a clear, neutral Colombian (Bogotá) accent.`
- **México · central (CDMX)**: `Spanish with a Mexican central, Mexico City accent.`
- **Latinoamérica · neutro**: `Spanish with a neutral Latin American accent.`

**Skincare**

⚠️ Esto le cuesta más a la IA que otras acciones. Abrir el producto (tapa): la 2ª imagen es solo una referencia de la tapa flip-top abierta. Es donde la IA más se confunde (tiende a arrancar el tapón en vez de abrir la bisagra); con esa foto entiende qué queremos y respeta el bote.

**Tapa abierta** (foto · 2 img)
```
Using the two reference images (image 1: the person holding the product bottle; image 2: an open flip-top cap, shown ONLY as a reference for the mechanism), keep the SAME person (same face, hair, skin and identity), the SAME bottle with its label, colors and text exactly unchanged, and the SAME room, background and lighting.

## C. Los formatos

El anuncio ya montado. Combina las piezas de A (imagen) y B (vídeo), y añade la voz y el acento.

### UGC · 1 persona a cámara

**Para vídeo · mirando a cámara**

- **Cámara quieta**: `(static camera, no movement) he's/she's looking directly at the camera, realistic arm movements, clear influencer tone: "[inserta el guión aquí]"`
- **Con micro-movimientos**: `realistic arm movements, the woman/man says exactly with direct clear energy: "[inserta el guión aquí]"`

> Luego añade la voz (sección B), el acento y, si quieres, una micro-acción. Para poner el producto en la mano en la foto, ve a A · Imagen → Producto (Sosteniéndolo / Señalándolo).

**Mostrar el producto · en el vídeo**

**Lo sostiene y enseña**
```
Using the product from the reference image, the person holds the product in one hand and shows it to the camera while talking, looking at the camera with natural, realistic gestures. Keep the product's label and packaging clearly visible and unchanged. Single continuous take, no cuts.
```

**Lo señala y lo mira**
```
Using the product from the reference image, while talking to the camera the person naturally points at the product and glances down at it a couple of times, then back to the camera, with relaxed, realistic gestures. Keep the product's label and packaging clearly visible and unchanged. Single continuous take, no cuts.
```

**El producto solo · B-roll**
```
Using the product from the reference image, a clean beauty shot of the product on a [surface: marble counter / wooden table / neutral backdrop], slow gentle camera move, soft natural lighting, the product in sharp focus with its label clearly visible and unchanged. No people. Single continuous take, no cuts.
```

**Ejemplo completo de UGC** (guión + voz + acento combinados)
```
The person looks directly at the camera with natural gestures and a clear, friendly influencer tone. Slow zoom-in toward their face from the first frame, holding once close. Single continuous take, no cuts. They say: "[tu guión]"

Voice: a bright, energetic male voice – a guy in his mid-to-late 20s, clear and relaxed, friendly and upbeat.

Spanish with a neutral Peninsular (Madrid) accent, informal and conversational, like a real casual chat in Spain.
```

### Podcast · 2 personas

Generas a cada persona por separado (cámara quieta, que hable solo ella, su voz) y las unes en CapCut cortando entre las dos, como un podcast real. Para la segunda, voltea la imagen (A · imagen).

- **Que mire al interlocutor**: `She/he is looking to the right/left side of the frame, as if talking to someone sitting next to her/him.`
- **Cámara estática, hablando**: `(static camera, no movement) the person talks naturally with realistic micro-movements and subtle arm gestures, authentic conversational energy. They say: "[inserta el guión aquí]"`
- **Que hable solo una persona**: `Single speaker: only the person from the reference image talks.`

> Segundo invitado directo: genera al otro invitado en el mismo sitio pero del otro lado, sin voltear la imagen (así el fondo no sale calcado en espejo). Sube la foto del primer invitado como referencia.

**Segundo invitado** (foto)
```
Using this image only as a reference for the setting and style, recreate the SAME podcast in the SAME room – same kind of sofa, the same boom podcast microphone on its arm, the same cozy warm living-room atmosphere and a similar curtain backdrop – but seen from the OTHER side of the podcast, as if filming the second guest sitting across the table.
